Hello

This is my Leaving Certificate Computer Science project for 2025. The brief can be found here. My project has to do with traffic congestion data on all of the 10 public holidays from 2020-2024, except 2024 has some missing data.

The Pathfinding page contains a map with a density heatmap of congestion, the mean of the past years' congestion at each site, and a form, where you can input a start and end coordinates, and it'll find the best path according to the heatmap. If you wish you can comment on if it was useful or not, and your response will be stored and can be accessed via the Responses page.
Instructions to use:

  1. Select the holiday, start time and end time.
  2. Generate the heatmap.
  3. Input latitudes and longitudes for the start point and destination. This can be done by typing into the text boxes, or by selecting the Find On Map (or similar) buttons and clicking on the map.
  4. Create the path by clicking on Pathfind.
  5. If you wish, you can give feedback by saying if this tool was helpful. All previous fields must be filled in and valid

The Statistics page contains bar charts of the total traffic congestion volume for the previous years. There are also density heatmaps for each holiday and the years of which I have data for.

The Responses page contains the responses submitted from the Pathfinding page.

Basic Analysis

From my data I found out the following:

  • The most congested holiday is St Brigid's Day, with a average volume of 585 (585.1570155231623).
  • The least congested holiday is Christmas Day, with an average volume of 323 (323.0772621858889).
  • The most congested year was 2022, with an average volume of 495 (495.2509310406889).
  • The least congested year was 2020, with an average volume of 401 (400.70593595128565).
  • The most congested time range was 14:00 - 15:00, with an average volume of 773 (773.4855059448975).
  • The least congested time range was 04:00 - 05:00, with an average volume of 129 (128.94033352584464).
  • The most congested location was HOWTH RD @ BROOKWOOD AV, with an average volume of 3152 (3151.5457842248416).
  • The least congested location was AMIENS ST @ BUCKINGHAM ST, with an average volume of 0 (0.0).
  • The most volume passing through any site at any hour range was 454 (454.034642410126) at ABBEY ST @ MARLBOROUGH ST (LUAS) on 1/1/2020 at 0:00 to 1:00.

Note that some of the data may be inaccurate due to the limitations of the data.
